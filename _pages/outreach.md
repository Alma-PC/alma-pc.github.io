Outreach and Mentoring
======

I am passionate about making science accessible and inspiring the next generation of innovators. Through local schools and community organizations, I organize hands-on STEM workshops and lead interactive presentations that spark curiosity among K-12 students, especially those from underrepresented backgrounds. As a mentor, I have guided undergraduate research interns and new graduate students, offering personalized support to help them navigate academic challenges and reach their goals. I also take part in professional society mentoring programs, sharing career advice and fostering an inclusive environment where everyone feels valued. My approach centers on open communication, encouragement, and adapting to each mentee's needs; several of my mentees have gone on to pursue advanced degrees and careers in the field.

Science Communication
======

**Co-Founder, [Ciencia sin Jerga](https://www.youtube.com/@cienciasinjerga)**  

I co-founded and co-lead Ciencia sin Jerga, a science communication initiative that translates and shares recent scientific advances in Spanish. Our goal is to break down language barriers so that Spanish-speaking communities can access cutting-edge research. I co-lead a team that produces clear, engaging summaries of new discoveries, hosts webinars, and collaborates with scientists across disciplines to keep every message accurate and culturally relevant. This work has deepened my commitment to inclusive science communication and extended my impact beyond the laboratory and the classroom.

In my next role, I plan to keep building bridges between research and the communities it serves: mentoring early-career scientists, supporting programs that broaden participation in science, and continuing to communicate research in both English and Spanish.
