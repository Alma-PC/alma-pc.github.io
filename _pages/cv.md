Professional experience
======

**Doctoral Researcher, Cancer Genomics**  
*Carvajal-Carmona Lab, University of California, Davis | Davis, CA, US | Sep 2019 - Feb 2026*  

* Co-led genomic instability research in cancer patients, establishing several patient-derived cell lines and designing CRISPR/Cas9 models to map mutation burden and drug sensitivity.
* Co-authored gastric cancer disparities research, including preclinical studies of chemotherapies and targeted therapies and patient-derived models from racial/ethnic minority patients.
* Trained nine researchers in NGS library prep, 3D organoid culture and computational genomics workflows in R and Python.
* Led mutational signature analysis linking arsenic exposure to bladder cancer etiology, integrating tumor sequencing with epidemiological data and presenting findings at AACR and Stanford.
* Evaluated the somatic mutational landscape of tumor samples from patients exposed to arsenic in drinking water in Latino populations, and confirmed similar patterns in the TCGA bladder cancer dataset.
* Built analyses in R and Python that combine genomic and epidemiological data.
